1 · Quick revision

A network topology describes how devices are connected in a network. Study the diagrams below.

⭐ Star

Every device connects to a central switch. If the switch fails, the network is affected.

🚌 Bus

All devices share one main cable. A break in the main cable can stop communication.

💍 Ring

Each device connects to two neighbours, forming a loop. In a simple ring, a break may disrupt communication.
